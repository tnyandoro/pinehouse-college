Academics

Two curricula, one supportive school, Forms 1 to 6.

Cambridge International

Pinehouse College is a registered Cambridge International School. Learners progress through three stages:

Lower Secondary Checkpoint

The foundation stage, building skills in the core subjects.

IGCSE

An internationally recognised qualification taken at the end of the middle years.

GCE AS and A2

Advanced study in the upper school, preparing learners for university.

ZIMSEC

Learners who follow the national curriculum are prepared for ZIMSEC examinations by the same dedicated teachers.

Commercial courses

Practical commercial subjects give learners useful skills for the workplace and for further study.
